# Krabink privacy policy

Krabink does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Notes, sketches and the list of your paired devices (device name,
platform, last-seen time) are stored on your devices. When you pair
devices, that data syncs directly between them over an end-to-end
encrypted connection and, if you set one up, through a relay or replica
server that you run. None of it is sent to the developer or to any third
party.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash
reporting of its own and no tracking. The camera is used only to scan a
pairing QR code; the local network is used only to find your paired
devices. If you install the app through TestFlight, Apple may share
crash reports and feedback you choose to send with the developer under
Apple's own privacy policy.
